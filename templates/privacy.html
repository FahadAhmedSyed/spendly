Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. As you use the service, we collect the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with basic usage information needed to keep the app running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve Spendly: to display your expense history, generate spending summaries and category breakdowns, authenticate your account, and communicate with you about your account when necessary. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery services) to operate the application; these providers process data only as needed to provide their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
